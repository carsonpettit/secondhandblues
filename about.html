Behind the Blues

Hi! I'm Carson, it's nice to meet you!

I started Second Hand Blues after being fed up with fast fashion. I believe clothes are meant to last and to show your personal style. Much of the clothing mass-produced today is poorer quality and caters to trends designed to make us all look like everyone else.

My boycott of shopping lasted a mere few months before I realized: I actually love shopping. I just don't want to overpay for cheap material, and I often don't resonate with the newest, hottest trends.

That led me to start thrifting all my clothes. I love searching through the racks to find that diamond in the rough. Thrifting has let me experiment so much more and find pieces that truly feel like me, and I love giving clothes a brand-new life and a second chance at being worn.

When I found out a flea market in my area was looking for vendors, I knew I wanted to share that joy with others. Customers would come up to me and say, “I wish you had something like this top in blue,” and the next week I'd stumble on exactly what they were looking for. That made me want to help even more people find their perfect pieces, which is how personal style bundles were born.
